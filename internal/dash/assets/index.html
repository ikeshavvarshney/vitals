A bookmarklet, for pages this instance does not serve

Drag this link to your bookmarks bar, open any site, and click it. The page reports its vitals here, under a route named after its host.

vitals snapshot

  1. Load the page you want to measure, then click the bookmarklet before interacting with it, so its LCP is not already final.
  2. Use the page as a visitor would, then press Send to vitals in the panel that appears.
  3. A tab opens here and records the measurement. Nothing is configured and no cross-origin permission is involved: the payload travels in the URL fragment, which browsers never send to a server.

Loading, layout, and paint numbers are the browser's own, replayed from its buffer rather than re-measured. INP only sees interactions that happen after the bookmarklet runs, so a missing INP means unmeasured, not fast. This is one visit from your machine on your connection, which is a sample of one and not the site's field data.

Reading these numbers

Percentiles are approximate. Values are bucketed rather than sorted. Millisecond metrics carry up to 4.9% relative error; CLS carries 0.0025 absolute.

INP is approximated. This is the longest single event over 16ms, not the high percentile of interaction latency that real INP reports. It is pessimistic in the tail.

Buffered writes. Up to 2 seconds of samples are lost if the server is killed rather than shut down.
