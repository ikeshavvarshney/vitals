A page that moves under you

Content is inserted after load without space reserved for it.

What this page does Three blocks are inserted above this text at 400ms, 900ms, and 1,600ms, with no height reserved. Everything below jumps down each time, which is exactly what CLS measures. The shifts are spread out deliberately so they fall into more than one session window.

A layout shift is only counted when it was not caused by a recent user interaction. These are not: they happen on a timer, with nobody touching the page. That is the difference between a banner appearing because you clicked something, which is fine, and a banner appearing on its own while you are reading, which is not.

Reserving height for late content, with a fixed size or an aspect ratio, is the fix. This page deliberately does not.

Every page here loads /b.js, the 942-byte beacon, from this same server. Measurements are sent once, when you leave the page or switch tabs, so click through a few pages and then open the dashboard.
