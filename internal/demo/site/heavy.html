A page with a heavy hero image

The largest element takes a long time to paint.

What this page does It inlines a deliberately enormous SVG containing 2,600 shapes. The browser has to parse and rasterise all of them, which pushes LCP well past the good threshold. FCP is affected less, because the text above paints first.

The image above is inline SVG rather than a downloaded file, so this page is heavy without fetching anything from another origin. The cost is real parsing and painting work, which is what LCP measures.

Every page here loads /b.js, the 942-byte beacon, from this same server. Measurements are sent once, when you leave the page or switch tabs, so click through a few pages and then open the dashboard.
